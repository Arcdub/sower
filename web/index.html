Sower

The whole Bible on your phone. No account, no ads, nothing tracked.

Read it now in your browser Get the Android app

The reader works on any phone, with nothing to install. The app keeps the whole Bible on your phone and can hand itself to someone else.

Passing it on

1Send this page

Share the link in any chat app or write it down. Whoever opens it can start reading straight away, on any phone.

2Send the app itself

In the Android app, tap Pass it on, then pick WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, email, or anything else that sends a file. The app travels as one file.

3No internet at all

The app can also hand itself over by Bluetooth or Quick Share, phone to phone, with no connection of any kind. Useful where data is scarce or unwelcome.

What is inside

Two complete English translations, the World English Bible and the Berean Standard Bible, both public domain and freely shareable. Words of Jesus in red. Search, highlights, and a night reading mode. The Android app asks for no permissions and makes no network connections at all.
